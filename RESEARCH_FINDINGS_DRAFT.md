## 1. Idea

When a firm files an earnings 8-K (Item 2.02), the option market prices event uncertainty into the near-dated chain. The tested question is whether event-day implied uncertainty is miscalibrated relative to matched same-issuer ordinary days and whether it survives a fixed payoff expression. The notebook defines five tradable structures (`long_call`, `covered_call`, `protective_put`, `collar`, `cash_secured_put`) plus a synthetic `stock` reference, not a sixth trade. One structure was predeclared primary before outcomes: `cash_secured_put`, horizon 5, bucket 3–6m, 5% out-of-the-money. The intended mechanism is premium harvest after the event.

## 2. Data and timing

The cohort is earnings-tagged Item 2.02 8-Ks in the canonical September-2026 TOP_100 over **2024-01-01 to 2025-12-31**. The frozen source gate enrolled **130 events across 28 CIKs / 27 tickers**; controls are up to three ordinary days per event (390 controls). Entry is the **close of the first trading session strictly after the official filing date**, with acceptance no later than that close; filing-date based, not acceptance-adaptive. The panel mixes **92 pre-open and 38 post-close** acceptances, and **eight PepsiCo events accept on the calendar day before `filing_date`**, so news-to-entry offset is not uniform (a limitation). Missing or stale marks are never zero. The protocol, events, universe and six implementation files were frozen before the report stage (`EARNINGS_PAYOFF_FREEZE.json`); the outcome database is locked by checksum. No 2026 filing, out-of-sample window or judges window was opened (`oos_opened`, `judges_opened` false; `new_JEV_requests` 0).

## 4. Costs and robustness

Costs are **modeled assumptions, not quote fills**: fixed commission, 5% annual funding, per-side premium haircuts 0 / 0.05 / 0.10, plus a modeled zero-edge break-even haircut of **0.6951/side**. At a 10% haircut the primary edge is +0.000813, far under the 0.005 threshold, with no interval. The break-even haircut is a modeled paired-edge root, not an executable-capacity or fill estimate; capacity is unmeasured. Year split is unstable: 2024 +0.001379 vs 2025 −0.000115; leaving the largest issuer out gives +0.001245. Robustness limits are structural: spot is inferred from put-call parity with a flat carry and **no dividend term**, and prices are **last-trade marks with no NBBO**, so a quiet leg can carry a stale print; the net number is a modeled scenario, not an executable fill.

## 5. Replication and limits

The frozen hashes and outcome lock verify. This is in-sample and exploratory, not independent confirmation.
