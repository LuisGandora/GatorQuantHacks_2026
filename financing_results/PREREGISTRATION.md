# FIN1: completed debt issuance and covered calls

Registered before financing outcome inspection. One primary hypothesis; no search across strategies.

**Hypothesis.** After text-confirmed completion of conventional debt issuance, a starter 5% OTM covered call adds more mean net value over stock alone than the same overlay on matched ordinary days. The primary horizon is 21 trading sessions; the predicted difference is positive. The economic conjecture is that the financing completion is not an operating catalyst, while call premiums overstate subsequent upside. Market efficiency could remove this effect before entry; this is a testable conjecture, not an established pricing error.

**Text feasibility audit.** The teammate CSV contains 423 debt/underwriting rows, combined into 292 company/accession transactions. A literal completion/debt screen, complex-security exclusion and seven-day repeat exclusion leave 124 candidates across 50 companies. The screen excludes press-release issuance where completion is not stated, convertible/exchangeable and equity units, acquisition/merger or separation text, structured/contingent transactions and explicit distress. This is not a refinancing claim; leverage, proceeds use and complete original filings are not independently verified. The CSV's JEV scores/labels are ignored. Financing subsidiaries are included when mapped to their listed parent. Both tags on one filing count once.

**Data and entry.** Reuse the starter universe, caching, expiry/strike selection, 2024–2025 in-sample and January–August 2026 historical validation windows. The CSV supplies in-sample excerpts; the Massive API supplies validation excerpts with identical rules. Verify exact taxonomy tags. Enter at the first trading-session close strictly after filing date, which handles after-hours filings conservatively. Earlier public announcements may already be priced. Actual unadjusted stock bars provide the benchmark; stock-split holding periods are excluded.

**Primary construction and comparator.** One standard call against 100 shares; 90–180 calendar-day expiry bucket targeting 120 days; starter 5% OTM strike algorithm. Require an entry-day call trade. Incremental gross value over identical stock is initial premium minus closing call mark, divided by entry stock price. At 21 sessions, the closing mark includes time value. Same company, year, quarter, weekday and 63-session radius; DTE tolerance seven days; nearest three usable ordinary controls, minimum one. Primary ordinary days exclude any tagged disclosure within 30 calendar days using the complete API calendar, not the partial CSV.

**Sensitivity.** Every fixed horizon 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 21, 42, 63 and expiry close. Original 1m/2m/3–6m buckets; 3/5/10% OTM calls; entry delay zero/one; exit marks no more than zero/three sessions stale. Costs zero/2.5/5/10% of option premium per side plus $0.65 per contract per side. Cleaner-entry ordinary sensitivity excludes disclosure within one session and financing disclosures within 30 calendar days; it does not replace primary matching. Record stock absolute movement and upside/downside tails, entry premium, closing value, earnings proximity, and capacity from the minimum daily option volume at entry/exit. No quotes, actual bid/ask fills, assignment or funding model is available.

**Inference and gates.** Count before pricing and usable events before inference. Forty eligible candidates are required to price; forty primary usable in-sample events are required to call a result worth pursuing as a statistical lead. Primary 95% intervals resample connected components linking repeated companies, reused controls and overlapping holdings; fewer than five components cannot produce a reliable primary interval. Company-only intervals are a secondary diagnostic and do not handle synchronized cross-company overlap. Drop the three largest positive contributions and report leave-one-company-out means. No horizon or parameter is selected to rescue the hypothesis.

**Validation and decision.** Freeze source, rules and data hashes before the first financing outcome. One financing validation look after in-sample completion; no further tuning. Supported requires positive primary intervals in both windows, adequate sample and positive drop-best-three mean. Contradicted requires negative primary intervals in both windows. Otherwise inconclusive, including a data-feasibility failure. These historical dates have been researched for other categories, so validation is not a pristine sealed test. Predict sealed-window fragility: low event coverage, already-priced transactions, earnings/macro confounding and trading costs may eliminate any apparent edge.

**Research history.** Earlier leadership tests were inconclusive and had adverse in-sample estimates. The team ledger reported 24 comparisons when this experiment began. This is a separate frozen experiment and does not alter the human-owned harness, notebook, JEV or ledger. Report all results including inconvenient exclusions.
