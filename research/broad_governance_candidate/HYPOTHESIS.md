# Broad-universe routine-recap covered calls: feasibility pending

**Hypothesis:** After routine affirmative annual-meeting recaps, starter 5% OTM covered calls add more mean net value relative to stock alone than on comparable ordinary days because the upside surrendered is limited relative to the premium collected.

This is a falsifiable trading hypothesis, not an assumption that routine meetings cause flat returns. Test call premium, subsequent positive stock movements and upside tails, short-call net value, spreads/commissions and capital/liquidity separately. Lower upside without adequate call premium fails the thesis. A valid negative independently estimated result contradicts the prediction; inadequate coverage or uncertain estimates remain inconclusive.

The user authorized broader companies and windows. The candidate universe is historically mapped annual-meeting filers outside the previously studied starter TOP_100. Historical equity types and standard listed options must be verified at entry, rather than requiring companies to survive to today. Newly expanded 2024–2025 companies have uninspected strategy outcomes for this experiment, but this is a historical post-discovery study, not a judges sealed replication.

Discovery: March 7, 2022–December 31, 2023. Validation: January 1, 2024–December 31, 2025. The exact tag is `annual_meeting_results`; excerpt flags require affirmative election/ratification/approval and exclude adverse/unknown text or consequential charter/bylaw/control language. These are provisional labels, not proof that investors expected every outcome. Full-filing text/expectations review remains required before any return test.

Enter at the first trading close strictly after the date-only disclosure. Preserve starter 3–6-month expiry and 5% OTM call-strike algorithms. Preserve original all-disclosure ±30-day ordinary controls, issuer/year/quarter/weekday matching, <=63-session distance, nearest three usable controls, and DTE tolerance. The primary horizon is 21 sessions; every fixed horizon and expiry remains required in eventual results. Use bid/ask execution and commissions, observed stock prices, split exclusions and displayed capacity.

One pre-quote event per ticker per window is selected by deterministic accession/date hash, with event/control primary holding intervals contained in the same quarter. This prevents holding-interval overlap between quarters and addresses the old transitive dependence problem. **CIK/share-class audit is still required** to verify that ticker-level selection really represents distinct companies; the current calendar counts are upper bounds, not final independently verified samples.
